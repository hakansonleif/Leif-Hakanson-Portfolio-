Hormuz: War-Risk Cost and the Economics of Transit

Leif Hakanson · BSc International Hospitality Management · EHL Lausanne · 2026 · 19 pages · October 2026

Download PDF Open in a new tab

What war risk costs a tanker crossing the Strait of Hormuz, and when sending the oil around it is cheaper. At normal freight rates, crossing wins. At today's rates the bypass wins wherever it has room, and it has room for only a fraction of the flow.

$4.28Expected war-risk premium per barrel
$9.01Bypass cost per barrel (Yanbu, SUMED, Cape)
$4.73Freight per barrel above which the bypass wins
13% to 28%Of pre-war flow the bypass pipelines can carry
