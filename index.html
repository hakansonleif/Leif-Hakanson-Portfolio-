Corporate Finance · Econometrics

Monday Effect: FTSE MIB Anomaly Study

EHL · Academic

Empirical OLS regression testing the Monday Effect anomaly in Italian equity markets. Sourced 15 years of FTSE MIB daily data from LSEG Refinitiv, cleaned holiday carry-forward errors, ran day-of-week dummy regressions. Effect attenuated to near-zero by 2020–2025.
